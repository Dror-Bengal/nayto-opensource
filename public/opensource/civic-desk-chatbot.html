Civic Desk Chatbot

Answer visitor questions on the web, WhatsApp and Telegram. Each channel keeps its own conversation history for follow-up questions.

A visitor asks when the next meeting takes place, then asks about step-free access. The chat answers both questions in the context of the same meeting.
